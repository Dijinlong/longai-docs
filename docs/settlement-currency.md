# Settlement currency: why your invoice does not match the price page

Display and settlement are separate facts, and most reconciliation problems start there.

## What the price page shows

A price page quotes a display currency. It may quote USD while settling in CNY, or quote CNY while settling in USD. The number on the page is a reference, not the amount debited.

## What actually leaves your balance

Settlement happens in one currency at a rate stated somewhere on the site. If the rate is not stated, it is applied anyway. Two consequences follow:

- A 3 percent exchange spread applied monthly is larger than most of the channel discounts people chase.
- Refunds and adjustments are usually settled at the rate of the original transaction, not the current rate.

## How to check before you pay

- Read the settlement currency on the [pricing page](https://ai.dijinlong.com/pricing) and the [FAQ](https://ai.dijinlong.com/faq).
- Compare the Chinese site and the [English site](https://ai.dijinlong.com/en): if one quotes CNY and the other USD, the settlement currency and rate should be stated on both.
- Reconcile one small top-up before committing a large one. The first invoice tells you the effective rate better than any page.
